# 核心阅读（按“离本研究有多近”排序）

## A. HCI 基础

1. **Shneiderman, B. (1983). Direct Manipulation: A Step Beyond Programming Languages.**  
   DOI: 10.1109/MC.1983.1654471  
   用途：可见对象、直接操作、让计算机“透明”。

2. **Hutchins, E. L., Hollan, J. D., & Norman, D. A. (1985). Direct Manipulation Interfaces.**  
   https://vis.csail.mit.edu/classes/6.859/readings/pdfs/Hutchins-DirectManipulationInterfaces.pdf  
   用途：从 cognitive distance 解释 directness。

3. **Carroll, J. M., Mack, R. L., & Kellogg, W. A. (1988). Interface Metaphors and User Interface Design.**  
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/B9780444705365500087  
   用途：metaphor 作为 initial familiarity 与 mental model 工具。

4. **Card, S. K., Moran, T. P., & Newell, A. (1983). The Psychology of Human-Computer Interaction.**  
   https://www.routledge.com/The-Psychology-of-Human-Computer-Interaction/Card-Moran-Newell/p/book/9780898598599  
   用途：HCI 的信息加工/工程模型基础。

## B. UI 历史与设计原始资料

5. **Lisa User Interface Standards (1980).**  
   https://guidebookgallery.org/articles/lisauserinterfacestandards  
   用途：simplicity、integration、familiar concepts、consistency。

6. **Sullivan, K. (1996). The Windows 95 User Interface: A Case Study in Usability Engineering.**  
   https://chi1996.acm.org/proceedings/desbrief/Sullivan/kds_txt.html  
   用途：Beginner Shell、Start、Taskbar、真实 usability iteration。

7. **Apple legacy iOS HIG: Design with the User in Mind.**  
   https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/referencelibrary/GettingStarted/RoadMapiOS-Legacy/chapters/RM_iHIG_Station/Fundamentals/Fundamentals.html  
   用途：consistency、direct manipulation、feedback、metaphor。

8. **Apple iOS 7 UI Transition Guide.**  
   https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/UserExperience/Conceptual/TransitionGuide/  
   用途：physicality muted、realistic motion、Deference/Clarity/Depth。

9. **Microsoft Metro Design Principles.**  
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/archive/msdn-magazine/2012/january/windows-phone-design-your-windows-phone-apps-to-sell  
   用途：content not chrome、authentically digital。

10. **Google: Material Design — A Look Back at Its Beginning.**  
    https://design.google/library/material-design-launch-2014  
    用途：纸张、光影、运动 → design rules。

## C. Skeuomorphism / flat 的实证限制

11. **Oswald, D., & Kolb, S. (2014). Flat Design vs. Skeuomorphism.**  
    https://cognitive.designsociety.org/publication/35915/  

12. **Oswald, D. (2018). Affordances and Metaphors Revisited.**  
    https://www.scienceopen.com/hosted-document?doi=10.14236%2Fewic%2FHCI2018.57  
    用途：提醒“拟物天然更好学”缺乏稳定证据。

13. **From skeuomorphism to flat design: age-related differences... (2020).**  
    DOI: 10.1080/0144929X.2020.1814867  
    用途：older adults 在 flat 任务中的表现限制。
